## How Trinkets Work in Welldweller

At their core, trinkets are equippable passives. You slot them into a limited number of slots, and their effects apply for as long as they stay equipped. Because slots are scarce, every pick is a trade-off — and that scarcity is exactly what makes the collection worth chasing.

### Rarity Is a Hint, Not a Verdict

Rarity tiers generally track raw power, but they do not track fit. A high-rarity trinket that boosts a stat your build ignores is worse than a common one that plugs a real gap. Community reports consistently show the same pattern: players who chase the shiny drop lose to players who chase the right drop.

### Stacking and Diminishing Returns

Most effects stack, but not linearly. Stacking the same archetype across every slot tends to hit diminishing returns quickly, while mixing two or three archetypes usually produces a stronger total output. Think of your slots as a small portfolio rather than a single bet.

## FAQ

**How many trinkets are there in total?**
The exact count shifts as the game is updated, so rather than quoting a fixed number, focus on the archetype table above. If you can identify which category a trinket belongs to and whether it fills a gap in your build, you are effectively tracking welldweller all trinkets without needing a rigid list.

**What is the fastest way to farm trinkets?**
Equip luck and economy trinkets, clear every optional room before advancing, and always buy from vendors when you can afford it. Community reports suggest luck stacking during exploration is the single biggest time-saver, because it increases the number of rolls rather than the quality of any one drop.

**Are rare trinkets always better?**
No. Rarity usually indicates raw power, but power only matters if it matches your build. A common trinket that fixes a real weakness will outperform a rare one that duplicates a strength you already have.

**Can I lose trinkets between runs?**
This depends on how the game handles persistent versus run-based progression. In most setups, anything banked or crafted into your permanent collection is safe, while items picked up during a run stay with that run. Bank early and often if you are unsure.

**Do I need every trinket to beat the game?**
Not at all. A focused loadout built from a handful of well-understood trinkets is enough for a clean clear. Full completion is for players who enjoy optimization, not a requirement for finishing.
